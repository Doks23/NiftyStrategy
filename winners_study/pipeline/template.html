    Read

    Quarterly results (YoY)

    Result date = quarter end + 45 days (+60 for March), the SEBI deadline. Actual dates are often earlier. “TA” = loss → profit. During replay, quarters not yet reported are faded.

    Relative-strength story

      Relative strength = stock price ÷ Nifty Smallcap 250, so it rises whenever the stock beats the index, even on days when both fall. This panel uses that ratio; the chart no longer draws it.

      Big days: market or stock?

      The biggest moves from 4 months before the low through the 52-week-high reclaim, plus days when the stock rose while the index fell 1.5% or more. Excess = stock % − Smallcap 250 %. Click a row to jump the chart to that day.

      Review

      Marked as not winners

      Use Mark as not a winner in a stock's Setup score panel for moves that are not real setups: a takeover, a price-only or operator-style move, or bad data. Marked stocks drop out of every summary on this page right away. To remove them from the study for good, ask Claude to “remove the marked stocks”.

      Turn it into a scan

      Draft screening rules from the 2025 pattern

      Watchlist: during a market correction

      1. Smallcap 250 is ≥15% off its high (the 2025 drawdown was ~25%).
      2. The stock is ≥30% off its 52-week high (median: ).
      3. On the index's retest low, the stock holds a higher low ( with enough history).
      4. Median daily turnover ≥ ₹1 cr. Below that, fills and exits are hard ( failed this).

      Trigger: the first entry

      1. The stock is ≥15 points ahead of the index since its low (median: ).
      2. It closes above its highest high of the last 50 sessions on ≥2× the 50-day average volume ().
      3. Up-day volume beats down-day volume over the prior 50 sessions ().
      4. Typical timing: about days after the low, with already gained off the low.
      5. Check the market first:

      Confirmation: add or enter late

      1. It closes above its 52-week high ( did), at a median × volume.
      2. Close above the 50-day average, which is above the 200-day, with the RS line at a 52-week high ().
      3. The latest quarter shows net profit +25% YoY or a loss-to-profit turn ( had one of these).

      Red flags seen in the winners anyway

      1. Price ran ahead of earnings: CUPID's results were weak when it broke out, and the profit growth came 2–3 quarters later.
      2. Moves on corporate events, not earnings: SMLMAH (takeover by Mahindra) and JINDALPHOT (holding company).
      3. Falling earnings with rising price, which can look like operator activity: SINDHUTRAD, AAREYDRUGS. Track these, but size them small.
      Method and limits
      • Move = the largest rise from a 2025 low to a later 2025 high, from Dhan prices. For the original 32 it matches the input file except SMLMAH and SILGO (likely a different price basis). The 50 later additions were screened from a second file: duplicates, non-shares and names that moved under 100% in 2025 were removed (see inputs/winnerdb_2025_screening.csv).
      • Tiers: Tier 1 = 2025 move ≥ 200% (33 stocks), Tier 2 = 100–200% (49). The red-flag examples are from Tier 1.
      • Breakout = the first close after the low above the prior 50-session high, on ≥1.5× the 50-day average volume. Reclaim = the first close above the prior 250-session high.
      • RS = stock close ÷ Nifty Smallcap 250 close, scaled to 100. Recent listings (for example QPOWER, ATHERENERG, BHARATSE, KMEW) have short histories, so some long-average checks show as n/a.
      • Winners only. There is no comparison group, so a hit rate here does not show how often the setup works.
      • Earnings come from Screener's quarterly table (consolidated where available). Result dates are estimated.
